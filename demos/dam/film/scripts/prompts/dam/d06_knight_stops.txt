EPIC|At a stone gatehouse at night, a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem holds up a parchment letter with a bright crimson wax seal to the light of a brass lantern held by a lantern-bearer in a hooded grey cloak, and raises a gauntleted hand to stop a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap at the gate. The squire waits calmly; other plain letters in his satchel are fine. Firm but fair mood. The characters are on the left half of the frame, the dark misty road on the right.
