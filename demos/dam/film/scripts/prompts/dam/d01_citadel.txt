EPIC|A vast stone castle on a hill at night, seen from afar: dozens of small lit tower windows, each a warm workshop where a a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap works at a desk. Every road from the castle leads to one great fortified gatehouse with torches, where gate wardens in long blue-grey livery coats with silver trim, each with a large ring of iron keys at the belt stand watch. Moonlight, mist in the valley, deep teal sky. The castle fills the right two thirds of the frame, soft empty sky on the upper left.
