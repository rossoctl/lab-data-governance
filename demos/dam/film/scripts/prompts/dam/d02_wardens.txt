EPIC|Inside a great torch-lit stone gatehouse at night, gate wardens in long blue-grey livery coats with silver trim, each with a large ring of iron keys at the belt unlock a heavy iron-banded gate for a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap who carries a satchel of sealed parchment letters. Beside the gate hangs a plain wooden board carved only with simple arrow shapes and small round painted symbols, with no lettering at all. Orderly, protective, reassuring mood. The wardens and squire are on the left half of the frame; the right half is a dim stone wall with soft empty space.
