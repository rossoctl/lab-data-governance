EPIC|At the open gate of a stone gatehouse at night, a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap walks out onto a misty road carrying a bundle of parchment letters, one sealed with a bright crimson wax seal, and an iron key from the castle. Behind him gate wardens in long blue-grey livery coats with silver trim, each with a large ring of iron keys at the belt wave him through after reading the signpost. The squire is on the right third of the frame walking away, the gate on the left.
