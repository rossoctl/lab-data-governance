plug|This is where Sentry comes in.
plug|Sentry has two components: aiac, automated access control management, and dg, data governance.
plug|Sentry plugs into DAM's gateway through a new policy enforcement point.
plug|Each tool call is checked there, before the agent's token is attached.

aiac|aiac's onboarding process reads the agent's description and automatically writes its policy to conform with the agent's intent.
aiac|Three tools are allowed: issue read, list issues, and search issues. The other forty-three are denied.
aiac|Here is the policy aiac generated, in Rego, running in OPA. {hold=2}
