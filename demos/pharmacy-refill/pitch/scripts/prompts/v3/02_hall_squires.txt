EPIC|Inside a grand torch-lit stone castle hall with tall gothic arches, several squires, each a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap, hurry between the archways carrying sealed parchment letters and handing them to one another. On a raised stone gallery at the back, a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem watches over them. Warm torchlight, deep teal shadows, busy and purposeful mood.
