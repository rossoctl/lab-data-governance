EPIC|Inside a stone castle gatehouse tunnel at night, a heavy black iron portcullis with sharp spikes slams down to the ground, sealing the archway shut, sparks flying where its spikes strike the stone floor. In front of it a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem stands firm with shield raised. Torches burn on the walls, cold blue light from the moat outside glows through the iron bars.
