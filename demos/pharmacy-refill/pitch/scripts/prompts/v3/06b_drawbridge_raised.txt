EPIC|Front view of a massive stone castle gatehouse at night across a wide moat of dark water glowing faintly blue. The gate opening is completely sealed by a heavy wooden drawbridge raised fully upright, standing vertical and flat against the stone wall like a closed wooden door, iron chains pulled tight from its top corners up into the wall. There is no bridge across the water, only open moat between the viewer and the closed gate. On the battlement above stands a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem with shield raised. Torches blaze, mist rises from the water.
