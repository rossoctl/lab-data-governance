EPIC|A long dark stone castle corridor at night lit by small wall torches, deep teal shadows with warm golden accents. In the right foreground a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem raises a glowing brass lantern high and looks back down the corridor. Along the corridor four squires, each a young squire in a teal tabard over a brown padded tunic with a simple round steel cap, stand spaced apart in a line receding into the distance. At the far end of the corridor a large open book glows softly on a wooden lectern.
