EPIC|A massive stone castle gatehouse at night seen from across a wide moat glowing faintly blue. The heavy wooden drawbridge is pulled up vertical and shut tight against the gate, its iron chains taut. On the battlement above the gate stands a tall knight in polished steel plate armour with a closed great helm, a deep crimson surcoat and cape, and a tall shield with a golden emblem with shield raised. Torches blaze, mist rises from the water. Dramatic, decisive mood.
